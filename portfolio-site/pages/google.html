Google Cloud, 2019–2021

Role: Design Lead, Enterprise Communications

Scope: Cloud infrastructure, AI transformation

Teams: Product, marketing, sales

Cloud infrastructure and AI capabilities needed one coherent story across product, marketing, and sales. Each function told it differently, opening interpretation gaps between technical capability and customer communication, and every campaign began with custom translation.

The reframe

Three functions were not disagreeing about the product. They were missing a shared language for it. Give them the structure and the translation work disappears.
