About

I'm a creative director and systems designer who builds reusable tools and shared infrastructure that let distributed teams move faster without losing judgment.

Over the past 15 years, I've led design systems and creative direction at Apple, Google Cloud, Vevo, Tribeca Film Festival, Nickelodeon, and Cartoon Network.

My work focuses on building creative systems that others can use — clear decision rules, reusable modules, and governance structures that maintain quality at scale while allowing teams to move independently.

I also run Field of Action, a practice exploring relational design, systems thinking, and the intersection of method and craft.
