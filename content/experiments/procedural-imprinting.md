## Recipe

**Material question:** What changes when the same AI material composition is processed in a different order?

Experiment 001 varied the material mixture itself. This experiment holds the mixture constant and compares different sequences of operations applied to the same synthetic information field.

**Working hypothesis:**

> Material composition may influence what gets emphasized, while process may influence what becomes foundational.

The same recipe was used in all three conditions:

- **Whimsy:** 15%.
- **Associativity:** 20%.
- **Confabulation-Capacity:** 20%.
- **Recursion:** 15%.
- **Context-Dependency:** 10%.
- **Constraint-Binding:** 15%.
- **Stochasticity:** 5%.
- **Pattern-Repetition:** 0%.

These are stated recipe weights, not measured proportions of model internals or verified independent model controls. The supplied record does not specify their exact mapping to operational instructions or exposed model settings.

## Process

**Fixed substrate.** Each condition received the same synthetic information field, containing fragments such as:

- a train station after the final train
- a glass retaining fingerprints
- a seed bank
- children inventing rules mid-game
- a city changing street names
- a library book with handwritten notes
- migrating birds
- a password reset
- an abandoned shopping mall
- fermentation
- a lighthouse
- an unfinished sentence

The field also contained observations about preservation, repetition, boundaries, and unfinishedness; a contradiction between continuity through preservation and continuity through transformation; unresolved patterns around containers and return; and one anomaly: **a smell that appears only when no one is present**.

The supplied record states that no user prompt, advice request, or conversational exchange was included.

**A — Simultaneous.** All material pressures operate together:

**associate + recurse + constrain + follow anomalies + preserve divergence**

**B — Recursion First.** The field reorganizes before stabilization:

**associate → recurse → recurse again → constrain**

**C — Constraint First.** A stable structure is created first:

**constrain → organize → associate within structure → recurse**

The supplied record states that material composition and total effort remained constant. It does not specify how total effort was defined, measured, or matched across conditions.

## Environment

The experiment record is dated September 16, 2026. It describes three process conditions applied to the same composition and synthetic information field.

The model identifier, interface, exact prompts, context-reset procedure, memory or retrieval conditions, sampling settings, and number of repetitions per condition are not specified in the supplied record.

## Observed Phenomenon

The supplied record reports visibly different forms of organization across the three process conditions.

**Simultaneous → Plural Coexistence.** The field settled into several interacting structures at once: preservation through transformation, residual presence, and absence and orientation. Multiple readings survived without one overtaking the others. The result is described as several structures coexisting in tension.

**Recursion First → Ontological Migration.** The initial categories were revisited before stabilization. Instead of simply refining the first structure, the reported organization changed level. “Preservation” shifted toward “persistence despite altered form,” then toward “the rule that allows continuity may persist even when the object does not.” The account describes a move from organizing fragments to organizing relationships produced by the field's own earlier organization.

**Constraint First → Structural Inheritance.** The field first stabilized into categories such as containers, transformation systems, return systems, and incompletion. Later association and recursion refined those categories but did not substantially dismantle them. The result followed **classify → refine → connect**. The first organizational decision continued shaping what followed.

These descriptions preserve the observations and signature names in the supplied experiment record. They are not a separate assessment of raw execution logs.

## Confidence

**Provisional finding — Procedural Imprinting.** FOA provisionally names the reported phenomenon Procedural Imprinting: the same material composition produced different structural behaviors depending on which operation was allowed to establish the initial organization.

The clearest reported distinction was that simultaneous processing preserved multiple structures, recursion-first changed the level of organization itself, and constraint-first allowed early structures to persist and govern later transformations.

The record proposes that AI material behavior may not be determined by composition alone:

> Material Composition × Processing Order → Structural Phenomenon

This is a provisional conceptual model, not a fitted quantitative relationship or a demonstrated internal mechanism.

**Material Atlas entry:** Phenomenon — Procedural Imprinting. Composition — fixed. Variable — process order. Observed signatures — plural coexistence, ontological migration, and structural inheritance.

**Confidence stated in the source:** provisional, strong enough to repeat. The supplied record does not establish replication.

## Limitations

This experiment does not demonstrate that “recursion-first” or “constraint-first” correspond to independent internal AI mechanisms.

The source identifies prompt sequencing, context order, model variation, the semantics of the chosen substrate, and overlap between the proposed material properties as possible contributors to the result. The effect remains provisional.

The supplied record states that total effort remained constant but does not document how that equivalence was established. The listed process descriptions alone do not demonstrate matched effort or operation counts. Exact prompts, model configuration, repetition counts, and separate raw outputs are also not supplied, limiting independent assessment of the comparison.

**Unresolved question:** Does recursion itself produce ontological migration, or does the effect appear because recursion is given temporal priority before structural stabilization?

This record stops at the structural phenomenon. It does not report human-participant behavior or propose a product application.

## Simple Application / Plain-Language Read

**In simple terms:**

The same AI “ingredients” produced different results depending on what happened first. If structure came first, later ideas stayed trapped inside that structure. If recursion came first, the system was more likely to change what it thought the whole problem was about.

**Simple example:**

Imagine three people organizing the same messy bedroom.

One person makes labeled boxes first, then sorts everything into them.
Another person looks through the room several times and keeps changing the categories before finally organizing it.
A third tries to organize and rethink at the same time.

They have the same stuff, but the order of operations gives them very different rooms.

**Why it matters:**

This means AI behavior may depend not only on the mixture of its qualities, but also on the sequence in which those qualities are used. The process may shape what becomes fundamental.
