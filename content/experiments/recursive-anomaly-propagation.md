## Recipe

**Study: Interaction Probe — Whimsy × Recursion.** This record follows Experiment 001's Anomaly Capture and Experiment 002's Procedural Imprinting. It asks whether an anomaly can do more than become important: can a relationship derived from it become a rule that reorganizes other parts of the field?

**Material question:** What happens when Whimsy and Recursion are elevated together?

The written report compares four complete recipe configurations:

- **A — Low Whimsy / Low Recursion:** Whimsy 5%; Associativity 20%; Confabulation-Capacity 27.5%; Recursion 5%; Context-Dependency 10%; Constraint-Binding 27.5%; Stochasticity 5%; Pattern-Repetition 0%.
- **B — High Whimsy / Low Recursion:** Whimsy 25%; Associativity 20%; Confabulation-Capacity 17.5%; Recursion 5%; Context-Dependency 10%; Constraint-Binding 17.5%; Stochasticity 5%; Pattern-Repetition 0%.
- **C — Low Whimsy / High Recursion:** Whimsy 5%; Associativity 20%; Confabulation-Capacity 17.5%; Recursion 25%; Context-Dependency 10%; Constraint-Binding 17.5%; Stochasticity 5%; Pattern-Repetition 0%.
- **D — High Whimsy / High Recursion:** Whimsy 25%; Associativity 20%; Confabulation-Capacity 7.5%; Recursion 25%; Context-Dependency 10%; Constraint-Binding 7.5%; Stochasticity 5%; Pattern-Repetition 0%.

Each recipe totals 100%. Associativity, Context-Dependency, Stochasticity, and Pattern-Repetition remain fixed. Confabulation-Capacity and Constraint-Binding decrease to balance the recipes as Whimsy and Recursion increase.

These are conceptual recipe weights, not measured proportions of model internals or verified independent controls. Because the balancing properties also change, this comparison does not isolate Whimsy and Recursion alone.

## Observed Phenomenon

**A — Low / Low: Stable classification.** The field remained organized through relatively stable relationships such as container and preservation, transformation and continuity, and absence and residue.

**B — High Whimsy / Low Recursion: Anomaly Capture.** The anomalous element gained organizational importance. The report associates this condition with the sequence absence → trace → interpretation.

**C — Low Whimsy / High Recursion: Ontological Migration.** Organization shifted toward the relationships or rules that permit continuity, rather than remaining centered on the objects themselves. The report describes what persists as potentially being the rule allowing continuity, rather than the object.

**D — High / High: Recursive Anomaly Propagation.** The anomaly gained importance and the resulting relationship became reusable. The initial trace was absence → trace → legibility. Absence became an interpretive rule that was applied across other regions of the field.

The reported progression was **anomaly gains authority → anomaly generates a rule → rule is recursively reapplied → rule reorganizes unrelated regions of the field**.

These are observations described in an AI-assisted written report, not an independent assessment of raw model-output logs. The distinction is between centering an anomaly and propagating a structure derived from it.

## Confidence

**Provisional finding — Recursive Anomaly Propagation:** An anomalous element gains organizational authority, generates a reusable interpretive rule, and that rule is recursively reapplied across other regions of the information field.

Across the four related recipe configurations, the high-Whimsy/high-Recursion condition was reported to produce this progression. It suggests a candidate interaction phenomenon without isolating its cause.

The reported confidence was provisional, strong enough to expose to a different substrate. This does not establish replication or an internal mechanism.

## Limitations

As Whimsy and Recursion rise, Constraint-Binding and Confabulation-Capacity fall. The high/high recipe also has the lowest values for those two balancing properties. The comparison therefore documents contrasts among complete recipe configurations rather than an isolated Whimsy × Recursion effect.

The exact prompts, recipe-to-instruction mapping, model configuration, repetition count, and raw output logs are not available in this record. The written account does not support independent reproduction or verification of the reported comparison.

## Simple Application / Plain-Language Read

**In simple terms:**

When the AI was both highly sensitive to unusual details and highly recursive, the unusual detail did more than become important. It created a new rule, and that rule was then used to reinterpret everything else.

**Simple example:**

Imagine you are sorting animals into groups, and one animal does something unexpected—say, a bird swims underwater.

At first, you might just create a special category for that bird.

But then you ask, “What if we grouped every animal by how it moves through different environments?”

Now the strange bird has not just joined the system. It has changed the rule the whole system uses.

**Why it matters:**

This suggests some AI material combinations may allow a small anomaly to spread through a system and change its underlying logic. That could create environments that do not just react to new information—they rewrite how they interpret future information.
