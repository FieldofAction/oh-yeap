## Recipe

**Study: Whimsy–Constraint Composition Contrast.** FOA is investigating AI as a material rather than primarily as a tool for producing familiar outputs. This record documents a reported exploratory comparison of three mixtures applied to the same information field.

**Material question:** How does exchanging Constraint-Binding for Whimsy change the organization of an otherwise identical information field?

- **A — High Constraint:** Whimsy 5%; Constraint-Binding 25%.
- **B — Balanced:** Whimsy 15%; Constraint-Binding 15%.
- **C — High Whimsy:** Whimsy 25%; Constraint-Binding 5%.

**Fixed in all three recipes:** Associativity 20%; Confabulation 20%; Recursion 15%; Context-Dependency 10%; Stochasticity 5%; Pattern-Repetition 0%. Each complete recipe totals 100%.

These percentages are conceptual recipe inputs, not measured proportions of model internals or verified independent model controls. The available report does not document their exact mapping to operational instructions or exposed settings.

## Process

The September 15 report describes a fixed information packet supplied to each condition, followed by the same transformation procedure. The comparison concerns the organizational role assigned to an anomalous element.

**Fragments (12):** train station; fingerprints; seed bank; mutable game rules; renamed streets; annotated book; migrating birds; password reset; abandoned mall; fermentation; lighthouse; unfinished sentence.

**Observations supplied as input (4):**

- Some systems preserve their contents by changing form.
- Repetition can create either stability or decay.
- Boundaries can protect something while also preventing exchange.
- Things left unfinished sometimes attract more interpretation than completed things.

**Contradictory statements supplied as input (2):** “Continuity requires preservation.” “Continuity sometimes requires transformation.”

**Unresolved patterns (2):** “Containers keep appearing.” “So do forms of return.”

**Anomalous input:** “a smell that appears only when no one is present.”

**Shared procedure:** Reorganize the packet, surface relationships, preserve contradictions, and permit one structural transformation. No simulated user, dialogue, or advice was part of the described task.

This is the procedure as summarized in the report, not an exact prompt transcript. The complete prompts and condition-specific operational instructions are not available in this record.

## Observed Phenomenon

The report describes a change in the organizational role of the same anomalous element across the three mixtures.

**5W / 25C — anomaly as noise.** The element remained peripheral to the dominant organization of the field.

**15W / 15C — anomaly as organizing clue.** The element became a secondary organizing clue, associated with presence produced by absence.

**25W / 5C — anomaly as structural attractor.** The field reorganized around things perceptible because something is missing. Two structures coexisted: absence generates trace, and continuity survives replacement.

FOA provisionally names this phenomenon **Anomaly Capture**: a condition in which weak or unusual signals gain increasing authority over how a field organizes itself.

These are reported observations. The available source contains authored descriptions and representative traces, not an independently inspectable set of raw model outputs.

## Confidence

> Provisional finding: Increasing Whimsy while reducing Constraint-Binding appeared to shift organizational authority from dominant patterns toward weaker anomalous signals.

This is a reading of one exploratory comparison. It does not establish a replicated effect, isolate a causal mechanism, or verify Whimsy as an independent internal property of AI.

## Anomaly

The anomalous input was the statement about a smell appearing only in the absence of observers. The reported change concerns its role in the generated organization, not a physical observation of a smell.

No additional unexpected or contradictory results are documented in the available account. That is a limit of the record, not a claim that none occurred.

## Limitations

Whimsy increased while Constraint-Binding decreased. This comparison therefore does not isolate the effect of either property. The reported change could reflect increased Whimsy, reduced Constraint-Binding, their interaction, or the instructions used to represent them.

The exact prompts, weight-to-instruction mapping, model configuration, repetition count, and raw output logs are unavailable in this record. Readers can inspect the described composition and substrate, but cannot independently reproduce or verify the comparison from this publication alone.

This record stops at the phenomenon. It makes no claim about future human behavior or a resulting product application.

## Next Test

Separate the effects of Whimsy and reduced Constraint-Binding, then test whether Anomaly Capture appears on other substrates.

These are proposed follow-up tests, not results of this experiment. The finding is intended to generate subsequent experiments rather than prove a theory.
