## Recipe

**Study: Whimsy–Constraint Composition Contrast.** FOA is investigating AI as a material rather than primarily as a tool for producing familiar outputs. This experiment compared three proposed material mixtures applied to the same information field.

**Material question:** How does exchanging Constraint-Binding for Whimsy change the organization of an otherwise identical information field?

- **High Constraint:** Whimsy 5%; Constraint-Binding 25%.
- **Balanced:** Whimsy 15%; Constraint-Binding 15%.
- **High Whimsy:** Whimsy 25%; Constraint-Binding 5%.

## Observed Phenomenon

The supplied account describes a change in the organizational role of the same anomalous element across the three mixtures.

**5W / 25C — anomaly as noise.** The element remained peripheral to the dominant organization of the field.

**15W / 15C — anomaly as organizing clue.** The element became a secondary organizing clue.

**25W / 5C — anomaly as structural attractor.** The field reorganized around the element.

FOA provisionally names this phenomenon **Anomaly Capture**: a condition in which weak or unusual signals gain increasing authority over how a field organizes itself.

These descriptions are summaries, not verbatim output excerpts.

## Limitations

Whimsy increased while Constraint-Binding decreased. This comparison therefore does not isolate the effect of either property. The observed change could reflect increased Whimsy, reduced Constraint-Binding, their interaction, or the operational instructions used to represent them.

The experiment does not establish Whimsy as an independent internal property of AI. Stated recipe percentages do not, by themselves, establish independent controls inside a model.
