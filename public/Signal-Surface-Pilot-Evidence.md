## Coverage and retrieval limits

Covers cultural gathering and music, service and technology infrastructure, research participation, archives, everyday exchange, and observational research. English-language sources concentrate on US/UK and online settings. No firsthand participant observation or interviews were conducted. Operator descriptions are stronger evidence of intended format than experienced benefit. Two abstracts add behavioral evidence but remain historical, observational, and connected to platforms already sampled.

Are.na pages share an operator; Library of Things pages share an operator; Zooniverse pages share an operator. Citizen DJ is a Library of Congress project. The study on citizen science concerns Zooniverse. The gifting study is not direct evaluation of the current Buy Nothing app.

Attempted but excluded: parkrun About returned a fetch error; By the People About returned 403; a Library of Congress blog page failed to open. Search excerpts for these were not counted as inspected observations. Sources from Field Reading 001 were reused only where publicly reopened for this pass.

Undated pages establish current published descriptions at retrieval, not the date a practice began. No velocity, frequency trend, or population-level prevalence was measured.
